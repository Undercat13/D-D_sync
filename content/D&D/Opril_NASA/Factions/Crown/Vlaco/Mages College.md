### Description
- The college sits on the basalt cliffs on the north west tip of the Gargantuan Isle. There are only two physical paths to the college the southern coastal road and by sea. The college experiences a high amount of  rain and snow, to combat this the great wizards created a weather dome spell to shroud the college from divination and protect it from the elements.
- The college has many different fields of study but they all fall under one of the different schools of magic. Free minded individuals seek out the secrets of the arcane and the structure and wisdom of the professors courses.
- Each morning the tenured professors hold a hearing for informing the public of happenings in the world and in the school. Additionally the court is used for disciplinary trials.
