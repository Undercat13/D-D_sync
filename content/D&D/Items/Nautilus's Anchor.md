## Appearance
- This  massive weapon is made from a ships anchor. Its built from hard and heavy steel, requiring multiple men to lift and use. It stands at a towering 8ft and 6ft wide of dented and worn steel with simple leather wraps at it's base for a handle. A chain 5 ft. in length dangles from the eye at the pommel of the ___"Weapon".___
- This weapon's origin is unknown, its a crude excuse of weapon that brutally pins its foes under its sheer size and weight.

## Effect
- This massive super heavy war pick has a +2 bonus to attacks and damage with weapon. The weapon has the super heavy property requiring a 20 STR score to wield, while also increasing the damage to $2d8$.
	- Sinking Strike:
		- Upon a melee hit the target knocked prone.
		- After hitting a prone target, you can pin the target by leaving the weapon on them, effectively grappling them. The pinned creature may attempt to escape against a DC 10 + STR + PROF.
		- Due to the sheer size of the anchor the if pinned creature is large or smaller it gets half cover from ranged attacks.
- While wielding the anchor you cannot be moved unwillingly (movement that doesn't use your movement speed).
- The anchor has a button on the handle end. You can use an action to press the button, which causes the rod to become magically fixed in place. Until you or another creature uses an action to push the button again, the rod doesn't move, even if it is defying gravity. The rod can hold up to 8,000 pounds of weight. More weight causes the rod to deactivate and fall. A creature can use an action to make a DC 30 Strength check, moving the fixed rod up to 10 feet on a success.
