## Appearance
- Dull silver staff, that's head opens up to 3 sections that spiral inward. 
- A net is tied across the spiral, with glowing blue beads forming a make shift dream catcher. 
- 3 trails of blue beads hang off the head of staff that clink together making soft sounds of rain.

## Effect
Requires attunement by a Druid, Sorcerer or Wizard

**Storm Focus.** You gain a **+1 bonus to spell attack rolls** and you **ignore half cover** when making a spell attack with this staff.1

**Cloud Magic.** This staff holds **10 charges**. You can expend any number of charges to cast the following spells as an action. The staff regains $1d8+2$ charges at dawn.

### Cloud Magic Spells

| **Charges** | **Spell**               |
| ----------- | ----------------------- |
| 3           | Gust of Wind            |
| 4           | Thunderwave (3rd level) |
| 5           | Sleet Storm             |
| 7           | Ice Storm               |
| 9           | Cone of Cold            |
