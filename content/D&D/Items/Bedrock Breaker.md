## Appearance
- **Description Box (Flavor Text):** Forged from **flamestone, bedrock, and living roots** drawn from the Chamber Deep. Its core is a miniature earthquake generator, pulsing emerald arcane power. Powered by paired flamestone orbs that rotate with gravitational force, each shot is imbued with a crushing seismic pulse and a trail of jade-green glowing energy. When its ammunition hits, brittle stone or crystal will erupt into jagged ridges that can seal tunnels or reinforce carveable passages in an instant.

- The weapon was devised for the Stoneguard, protectors of the Chamber Deep's endless, maze-like caverns. It is one of a series of arcane tools able to instantly seal breaches or shape barriers from a distance, turning the battlefield itself into stone. Wielded by Stoneguard elites, the weapon ensures the security and shape of the Deep, the dwelling number of earth elementalist mages who divined the techniques to turn mundane gems and flamestone into magical craft and enchantment into new forms to endure and survive.

## Effect
- You gain a **+2 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this weapon. On a hit, the target takes an extra **$1d6$ bludgeoning damage** (or the damage type of the ammunition, if greater favor).

- **Jagged Rise.** The crossbow has **3 charges**. When you make an attack with this weapon, you can expend 1 charge to replace that attack with an arrow infused with elemental earth. Instead of targeting a creature, you target a point of dirt, sand, or stone within the weapon's normal range. WHen the arrow strikes, a jagged ridge of rock erupts in a straight line from that point in a direction you choose.
	- This attack summons a **$10$ foot square of rock** erupting in a straight line from the point in a direction you choose.
	- The ridge is **$10$ feet long, $5$ feet wide, and $5$ feet tall**.
	- Each creature in the line must make a **DC 16 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **$3d6$ piercing damage** and are restrained on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. The escape DC for restrained is 16.
	- Each $10$ foot section of the ridge has **AC 15** and **$30$ hit points**.

- The crossbow regains all expended charges **daily at dawn**.
