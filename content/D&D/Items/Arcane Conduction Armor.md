## Effect
Light Armor

Requires attunement by a Spellcaster

**AC.** 11 + spellcasting modifier

**Configuration Modes.** As a bonus action you can reconfigure this suit to change its features. You can reconfigure the suit once per round. You may choose one of following modes.
- **Base Mode.** You gain resistance to Force damage.    
- **Fortified Mode.** You gain a +2 bonus to your AC and your movement speed is halved.
- **Stealth Mode.** Whenever you make a Stealth check you can add your Intelligence modifier to the roll. Additionally, yo3u can use the Hide action as a bonus action. However, you can not cast spells in this mode.
- **Burst Mode.** You gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and your AC is reduced by 2. []
