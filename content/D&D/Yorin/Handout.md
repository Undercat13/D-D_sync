## Current Situation
It is a **Godless Age of Darkness and Death**.

This adventure starts on the continent of **Yorin** */yôr-ĭn/*, in a larger world called **Enquist** */EN-kwist/* in the year of 1529. Although characters/people can come from all over **Enquist** including the three other major continents: [[Amox]] */A-mocks/*, [[Idris]] */IH-driss/*, [[Opril]] */OH-prill/*.

A great evil has awakened with powers that surpass the gods; defeating the world’s bravest heroes and cascading the world into a dark age. Seeing their champions defeated, the gods abandoned the Prime Material plane to hide in their divine realms, fearful. Left unchecked, evil has risen and blight is consuming the world.

This evil is known only as [[The Carrion King]], just speaking its name is said to curse you.

Though the people of Yorin never gave up hope, as a deity [[All Mother]] rose to power. The last deity protecting Yorin, sheltering it from the rising evils in the world.

Yorin is believed to be the only realm free of the Carrion King’s influence. Recent contact with other continents and their kingdoms has ceased. The kingdoms’ leaders are in a panic;  What can be done against the rising tide of evil? Are we truly the last bastion of civilization in the world? 
Blight is growing in dark corners of Yorin, as the [[All Mother]]’s light fades. Sickness and diseases are rampant, bandit attacks on the rise, rumors of undeath travel on the frightened breath of common folk. People live sheltered in walled cities and towns hiding from the dark wilds that stretch between these safe havens.

People have come far and wide to live under the light of the All Mother, refugees in the streets tell horror stories of the world beyond: 
“An eternal night, undead walking the streets, portals to hell and the abyss, plagues, droughts, disaster level storms”. 
There isn’t enough room or food to feed all, tensions are high. Those in power are looking elsewhere for solutions. 
Cities swell and spill over with the displaced, their mouths thousands too many to be fed.

You are 6 level 1 adventurers, in the city [[Skive]] in a dive bar called [[Whiskey's Whiskers]]. You've signed on with a merchant Gerard to escort him and his ship to [[Lyndon]]. Word has spread of pirates claiming the waters as their own. No sane man-at-arms would take the job, so Gerard turned to adventurers.

## Religion
- The gods have abandoned the people, as a result people have looked elsewhere for faith. Some of those include: primordials, devils, and demons. Though one god manifested in their absence, the [[All Mother]].
- Many kingdoms and cities have outlawed religion other than the [[All Mother]]. People caught following other powers are cast out as heretics and in extreme circumstances executed.
- It's rare to still worship the old gods, but some still do; even if there is no effect.
- Some chose to beckon the creeping darkness, worshipping The Carrion King under the banner of [[Mors Altum]].

### [[All Mother]]
- Goddess of life, mother to all. Has always been a part of Yorin culture. Is seen as an overall good in the eyes of the ruling.
- The religion has multiple tenets each of which was represented by a devout follower acts. Defining them as a saint of that tenet which they represent. The common folk worship the all mother in the form of praying to a saint.

### [[Mors Altum]]
- Worships the coming tide of death and darkness. Is seen as a heresy a dangerous faith needing to be destroyed in the eyes of the ruling.
- Occult rituals beckon the [[The Carrion King]] to provide salvation for all who welcome them in the new age of dark.

- Brothers of Badak - Knights of [[Thalgrim]]
