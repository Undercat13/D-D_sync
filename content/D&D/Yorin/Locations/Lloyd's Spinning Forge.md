### Description
- Open octogon building on the corner lot, each side has different function, honing, shaping, cooling, forging, ect. Gears and tracks rotate the floor to connect the working side to the heat of the forge. It's controlled by a series of leavers attached to the forge.
- the center has a massive forge that spits embers and smoke into the air, it makes a roaring sound when the furnace doors are opened. Its at least 20 feet diameter.
- 3rd ring of [[Lyndon]]
#### Characters
- Lloyd - Massive middle aged gith man, with dark black hair and green robes with a yellow leather apron. Master Smith craftsmen, uses magic materials and flames to forge epic weapons. Doesn't have an inventory but can forge or repair weapons if given materials. Only takes jobs he cares about.
- Assistant Mia - Younger gith woman with black hair and a bob hair cut
