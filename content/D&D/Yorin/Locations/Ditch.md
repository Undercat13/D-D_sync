### Description
- Up scale tavern on the outer ring of [[Lyndon]]. 2 blocks in from the docks. Build made of white stone and dark stained jungle wood, shaped like a pentagon with a small yard out front filling the rectangular plot it sits on. Two paved paths lead into the double doors on the two street facing walls. The second story has a connected wrap around balcony, and what looks like a dining area on the flat roof.
- Inside: warm well lit tavern with polished dark grey stone floors. a small pentagon stage in the center of the room with a piano and tall stool on it. Booths line the left and right walls, with a staircase each on the back corners Bordering the bar and kitchen. a few round tables dot the open space in the center of the room.
- It smells of citrus and salt in here, rooms are expensive 5 gold a night but include 2 meals and open access to the neighboring bathhouse (Fi's spaw) a tabaxi owned spa
