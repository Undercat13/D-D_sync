### Description
- The greatest city in all of Yorin. A massive coastal river metropolis, capital of the [[Lodel]] Empire. A free city, welcoming all resulting in a medley of culture and life. Safe from the harsh winds and currents of the Ocean, Lyndon offers a warm deep harbors to accommodate all kinds of ships and fishing. It's rich in the natural resources from the surrounding [[Tyth Rainforest]] and has been slowly expanding into it, clearing land for its growing population.
- Lyndon is a sprawling circular metropolis of stone, segmented into different rings by height. Its roads and cliffs are made of large white stones shaped into rectangles. The rings ascend ~15ft each, into the highest center point a massive white spire shaped of a moon sliver curving toward the jungle --- Crescent Building. Its shadow cascading over its domain. Lyndon incorporates nature in having tons of small plots of coastal & jungle trees. Many cliff faces between rings are covered in jungle vine, its vibrant green a stark shift form the pale white stone.
- Most buildings are made on a base of white stone with the orange jungle wood providing the meat of the building. Richer and higher class buildings are usually 100 white stone and have many more windows.
- The closer to center the richer the buildings and people become. 
- 12 Gondola's have been set up to transport people and cargo from the various rings in the city to the center. If one were to the view the city from above the gondola's path would fan out like the marks on a clock. 
- Just below the [[Crescent Building]] the Sky port sits. Its a massive half circle building with its curve towards the strait, with many metal docks jutting out into the open air. Few Blimp ships sit atop the docks.
- Lyndon may be the greatest trade city in Yorin. If you can dream it a vendor sells it. Each transaction is taxed to support the government, most places include this with the price. With the only exception of slaves, which is banned in [[Lodel]].
- Lyndon has a rich history, but its defining mark is the adaptability of its people. For example Lyndon was one of the first cities to adopt the Sky port. Which catapulted [[Lodel]] ahead in the trade industry,
- Lyndon hosts the government of [[Lodel]] It's parliament works out of the [[Crescent Building]] in the sky district.
- The bustling city breeds all sorts of bad actors, but the cities own [[Steel Doves]] keep the peace. The order is vast, many Doves uphold order though different means.
- A major revenue of the city is it's ferry system. It provides transportation to all cities on the Lyndon Strait and the Sapphire Sea by boat.
- Being close to the [[Tyth Rainforest]] creates a strange dynamic in the city. Many shamans and druids live with the less fortunate on the outskirts of the city using resources from [[Tyth Rainforest]] to get by.
