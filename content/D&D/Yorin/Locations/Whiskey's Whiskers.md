### Description
- A run down wooden dive bar right next to the harbor. Run by a family of Tabaxi with the surname Wilker. Its a dimly lit bar with the aroma of tabacco smoke and citrus. Your foot steps stick to the darkwood flooring with an audible suction pop every step. Round uneven tables with stools fill the bar floor, creating a maze weaving to the bartop. Each table here is set with only a few candles and a full ash tray.
- A hightop bar interjects between the kitchen and the bar room
#### Characters

- Zarlos Wilker
- Trist Wilker
