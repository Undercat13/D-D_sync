### Description
- magic item, shop run by a kenku named Chloe
- in the third ring of [[Lyndon]]
- a beautiful glossy black stone dotted with glinting. Metal fragments makes up a square building with large win tinted windows. A sign made of brass letters reads "Apogee Goods"
- inside is cool blue lighting and large with tiles floors. A grey and gold rug mark the direct footpath to the counter straight across the room. It is much bigger on the inside, racks of weapons, isles of trinkets, manikins with armor, racks of robes, all can be found within.
- this place is fairly busy, you see other adventures, masked figures, nobles, errand servants, and even some golems.
#### Characters
- Wisp - at the counter sits a young elven man with long curly redhair and fair skin. Wearing a black tunic left untied at the revealing a gold chain under his shirt. He has a long face with green eyes.
- Chloe -  An  elderly **Kenku** who wears oversized, spectacles perched precariously on her beak. She constantly fidgets with a small, tarnished silver compass she claims "points to the nearest bargain."
