### Description
- A quiet city of the misty coast of the Graven Rise.  A trading hub and military base for [[Lodel]] that is perpetually covered in a blanket of mist.
- The is mist is a part of daily life, the city folk have adapted their lives around it. Important buildings have multiple floors with raised skywalks above the sea of mist. With faces constantly obscured, people place greater value on **voices, gait, and reputation**. A good reputation is paramount.
- The mist is appears to have a mind of it's own, it moves and swirls in patterns without rhyme or reason. It's origin is unknown some believe it comes from the hot springs while others believe it has more nefarious roots.
- Few of the arcane have even dedicated their craft to researching the mist.
- The [[Steel Doves]] use special bells on their armor to identify each other during a crisis. Many are equipped with magic to gust the mist away.
- The [[Wavebreakers]] have a significant military base at Mist Hollow, many ships and some fully dedicated to research.
- Known for its deep waters and natural harbor its a great hub for ships.
- Graven Rise is ripe with natural resources: wood, stone, metal, healing hot springs ect.
- The buildings are made of a pale tan granite and dark wood.
- Mist Hollow is home to many blue collar businesses: Miners, Loggers, Hunters, Farmers, cobblers, carpenters etc.
- There is a significant elder community here, many traveled to Mist Hollow for it's healing mists and hot springs.
- Upper class district called the ___The Cloudline___
	- Great stone buildings built above the mist 
- Redlight district called the ___Rungs___
	- Poorer older buildings made of wood and ladders
- Dangerous poison filled district called ___The Alchemist's Quarter:___
	- A segregated area where the toxic vapors are a known hazard. Only those with special filtration masks or immunity dare enter. Strange creatures mutated by the waste occasionally crawl out.
