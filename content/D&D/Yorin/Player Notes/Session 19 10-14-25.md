#### Where we left off
- party successfully retrieved the jade dragon fruit from a blight4ed clearing filled with blight beasts and toxic gas.
- During retrieval Thomas was bitten and contracted a Curse.
- Then the party found some respite in a clear far from the blight and began their rest for the evening.
- The party shared moments from their backstroies together and deeped their bonds.
- Celeste elected to keep watch over night, during so she noticed a pair of eyes watching her from a distance that later disappeared. Hours later she heard a magic Bloop sound that shook the trees and set out to investigate it. She found a watering hole with signs of a scuffle where an invisible creature was lurking with a silent jungle. It noticed her, when she misty stepped away it reaction attacked her compressing space. She then cast fly and flew back to the party. Where she woke eagrin to search for the beast. They followed some blood trail to a deboned Black panther, and returned to the campsite to a silent jungle.
