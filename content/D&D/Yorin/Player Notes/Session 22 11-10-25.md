#### Where we left off
- The party arrived at [[Mist Hollow]], followed a gentlemen named [[Lewel Stonebeard]] into the [[Fogged Tankard]].
- The party questioned about the [[D&D/Yorin/Locations/Surfing Samurai|Surfing Samurai]] and the [[Blight Ship of the Night]]. Learning about a bell ringer named [[Pierce]] who swears that he saw the ship.
- Lewel Stonebeard was hired to be a guide for the party, he led them to [[Shady Cuts]] where Thomas received a hair cut and shave.
- At the barber they learned that the [[D&D/Yorin/Locations/Surfing Samurai|Surfing Samurai]] was a tattoo shop above a raman joint named [[Rakkan]]. To get in you have to say the passphrase "Howie sent me".
- After which the party visited  [[Moore's Goods and Stuff]] and purchased 4 plague masks.
- Along the path they passed a bounty board which contained info about:
	- Lyle Muddy - missing 16 yr dwarf in mines deep delve mine - missing for weeks
	- Peanut Woodbriar - A magic study looking for 12 people in good health. 120g reward.
	- Twig's book club - Friday night at 8 pm at Twig's house, snacks included
	- Cricket - Needs guards for their cargo ship to [[Borst Citadel]]
	- Nebula - missing drow woman with glasses, kidnapped in the [[Rungs]]
- Thorne and Thomas went into the [[D&D/Yorin/Locations/Surfing Samurai|Surfing Samurai]] for some sneaky espionage, while getting a lock of hair tattooed on Thorne.
- A sketchy Drow entered named [[Skunk]], who could get you anything. And one time in primary school ate a toad the size of a football.
- It went awry when the high artist started  hearing hidden voices and crashed out.
- Thomas revealed himself as a talking spider which further escaladed the situation, almost to blows.
- Thorne finished the tattoo and was kicked out.
- On the street a Yellow warden and ward questioned the party about [[Nebula]], insults were thrown and they left empty handed with sore egos.
- The party received a flyer to see a band called [[The Reason]] perform a rock show at venue called [[The Dark Forest]].
- After dropping off Lewel Stonebeard the party attended the concert, where after the performance [[The Alchemist]] made his appearance, enthralling the party with hypnotizing music while exposing them to toxic  gas.
- After donning his mask Thomas put up a stinking cloud on the alchemist, Eagrin flew up towards the stage, Thorne tried to find [[The Alchemist]] in the cloud, and Celest cleared the air with a gust of wind.
- Left off with Thomas dropping his cloud.
