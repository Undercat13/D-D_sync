#### Where we left off
- Landed in [[Lyndon]], watched the package with the helm, put on a pallet to be sent tomorrow. Party was given the other half of the 600 gold by Gerard and he payed for them to stay at the [[Ditch]].
- They heard of the night speech by [[Pallen Glover]], speaking to his voters about the recent hardships they're facing, the over population the increase in illness with the plague spreading. He warns about false prophets and to continue to follow the light of the all mother as he lead in prayer showering the people with the all mothers protection. 
- Suddenly cultist attacked the square slaying the commoners and going after the Senator. One of the cultist donned the Winged helm and channeled a ritual and transformed two commoners into a mass of flesh and mouths.
