#### Where we left off
- Celeste consumed the souls left behind by the alchemist and gained new powers
- They party was detained and questioned by the guard captain Talrun.
- Yellow wardens Reynard & Ysabel interrogated the party, all but Celeste told the truth, after which the party was freed.
- Scratch a goblin was also mistakenly freed when Eagrin gave them a lollipop
- The party licked their wounds at the [[Fogged Tankard]], and decided to go back to the [[D&D/Yorin/Locations/Surfing Samurai|Surfing Samurai]] at night and learn more.
- The party broke in with reckless abandon killing one of the artists Cliff on accident.
- Inside the party found no clues just drugs and some gold.
- Some common folk saw the scene and ran after being freaked out by a talking monkey.
- The party rested back up and sought out the merchant [[Cricket]] to accept a job as guards for his cargo trip to [[Ilog]].
- At [[Lucky's Mall]] Eagrin spotted [[Nuncio L'nache]] where they learned more about the city [[Mist Hollow]] and The Reason's connection to the [[Brotherhood of Crows]]. They made a deal to meet back at [[Slush]] at noon where [[Nuncio L'nache]] will provide black mail for a wizard to study the mysterious magical pale paper bed.
