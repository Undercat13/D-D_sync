### Description
- Large coastal city set into the white granite cliffs that shelter the Emyna Cove shaded by the massive pines of the [[Pyjin Wood]]. Its ceruluan colored harbor is the second largest in Yorin. 
- The harbor itself was a marvel, a natural deepwater basin protected by towering, white granite cliffs on either side, carved and smoothed over millennia by the relentless caress of the ocean. Within its sheltered embrace, the masts of elven galleons, sleek and adorned with intricate carvings, swayed gently alongside smaller fishing vessels. The docks, crafted from ancient, moon-silvered timbers, hummed with quiet activity, yet never felt bustling, always maintaining an air of serene efficiency.
- The city rose in graceful tiers from the water's edge, its buildings sculpted from a pale, almost luminous stone that seemed to absorb and reflect the changing light of the sky. Acrana check 10+ these walls are enchanted with lingering runes such as stone shape and mending. 20+ there is a faint whispering from the stones could it be? the stone is conscious? Rooftops were often adorned with living greenery, meticulously cultivated gardens and flowering vines that cascaded down walls like emerald waterfalls. Instead of rigid streets, there were meandering pathways, often elevated, that wove through the city, offering breathtaking vistas of the cove and the endless expanse of the sea.
- Dominating they city scape is the [[Sikus College]], Its library/observatory perched on the highest promontory overlooking the massive walled campus. Not singular building but a collection of connected impressive spires made of the dazzling white granite with cerulean roofs.
- Two large octagon shaped buildings mark the famous indoor trading halls. Each multileveled each floor representing a different type of good. Both of which have skyports atop them, for quick access to goods.
