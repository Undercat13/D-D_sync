#### Where we left off
- Traveling to the [[Lyndon]] skyport the party was attacked by a group of assassins and mages. Their main objective to collect the helm.
- Morgrim enlarged himself and slayed many of the attackers while maintaining control of the helm. The party fled the scene before the guards could arrive.
- The party met Wendy - steward of Pallen, met the pilot Stew - pilot of the Senates airship
- Eagrin was woken up in the middle of the night by Stew, Stew showed him the growing blight in the south side of [[Tyth Rainforest]]. Eagrin freaked out as he could hear the enemy that wounded him despite being 1000 ft in the air. He tried to jump off after him the party talked him down.
- Then Thomas saw the blight put on the helmet, He quickly lost control and began turning into a shadow knight and began commanding the blight. Celeste's quick thinking tried to command Thomas, he spell failed as the room darkened, yellow eyes peered out from the corner of the room then a shadowed hand swatted the spell down countering it. Eventually Thomas hearing Celeste's cries awoke and took off the helmet but not before seeing his handprint and will  controlling the blight in the forest.
- The party arrived at [[Emyna Nalore]] early in the morning requestioned a carriage to take them to [[Sikus College]] seeking [[Archebold Vincent]]. They walked in on a disagreement between him and [[Gretchen Pendrale]]. Eagrin suspects Gretchen as a Thayan wizard due to her red robes. He believes he's seen her when she was a child, and tried to confront her. Archebold diffused the situation by drawing the party's attention giving Gretchen time to leave. After inspecting the helmet, Archebold agreed to study it but had class shortly and needed ingredients for the ritual. He tasked party with collecting 2 pounds of shells and 3 mud jellies.

	- the mage [[Elareal Arcanum]] is seeking strange repeatable instances of wild magic
	- rangers wanted to investigate the sudden death of old Pines in the [[Pyjin Wood]]
