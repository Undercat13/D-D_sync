#### Where we left off
- Players met each other at [[Whiskey's Whiskers]] had a drink and met their patron Gerard.
- The party set off on Gerard's ship **Swift Fish**  with it's captain Miguel. After sharing some rum Miguel gave each party member bug poison to pour on themselves. (Which was immediately forgotten)
- Thorne discovered a suspicious ominous sealed metal crate in the cargo hold. With Thomas help he took a peak inside seeing a black iron winged helm with dried blood on it. He deftly closed the crate leaving no marks.
- Attracted to the ship a swarm of stirges attacked the party.
- Thomas fell unconscious, but the swarm was exterminated. And he was helped up by Eagrin.
- The party slept through the night and awoke with the ship entering the [[Sapphire Sea]].
